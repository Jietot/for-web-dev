Spanish-Period (1521-1898)

The Spanish period began when Ferdinand Magellan arrived in the Philippines in 1521. Spanish colonization was established in 1565 under Miguel López de Legazpi.

    Important Facts:
  • Christianity spread throughout many parts of the country.
  • Spanish authorities established a colonial government.
  • Manila became an important center of trade.
  • Filipinos experienced forced labor and heavy taxation.
  • Filipino revolutionaries fought for independence in 1896.
