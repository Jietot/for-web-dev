About The Philippines

The Philippines is a country located in Southeast Asia, consisting of more than 7,600 islands. It is home to diverse cultures, languages, traditions, and natural wonders.

    Quick Facts:
  • Capital: Manila
  • Location: SouthEast Asia
  • Official Language: Filipino and English
  • Currency: Philippine Peso (PHP)
  • Major Island Groups: Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao
